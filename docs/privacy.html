Privacy policy

We don’t collect your data.

TypeParkinson has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The system keyboard never saves what you type. Information you choose to keep in the app stays on your device, with optional profile and keyboard sync through your own iCloud.

Effective October 23, 2026. Applies to the TypeParkinson app and keyboard for iPhone and iPad.

What stays on your device

To build your keyboard, the app keeps on your device:

  • Your touch profile — the hand you tap with, and measurements from the exercises: how far each tap landed from its target, how long touches lasted and how far they moved, how closely you followed a path, and how many corrections and differences there were when copying a set phrase.
  • Your keyboard settings — key size, touch correction, repeat handling, tap tolerance, correction buttons, and any “adjusted for today” setting.

The keyboard reads these settings from storage it shares with the app on your device. It can refine the numerical touch model as you type; it does not keep the words used to refine it.

History, medication and Speak

Daily checks, how typing felt, practice results, motion and voice measurements, and medication schedules and dose logs are stored locally. Practice stores results and the exercise phrase, rather than a recording of everything you type. Voice practice uses a temporary audio file to measure loudness and deletes it when the meter stops. Saved voice history contains measurements.

Saved phrases are text you deliberately choose to keep. Speak also keeps your eight most recently spoken phrases on the device; use Clear beside Said recently to remove them. This is separate from the system keyboard, which does not save typing. You can edit or delete saved phrases in Speak.

PDF and CSV reports are created on your device. A report leaves your device only if you choose a destination in the share sheet. Reports can contain personal measurements, medication names and dose times, so choose who you share them with.

The keyboard

The TypeParkinson keyboard does not ask for Full Access. Without it, iOS doesn’t let a keyboard use the network, so it cannot send what you type anywhere. The keyboard doesn’t save what you type. To make the Undo button work, it remembers your last few edits only while it is open, in memory, and forgets them when it closes.

Sign in with Apple and iCloud (optional)

You can use TypeParkinson without an account. If you choose Sign in with Apple:

  • Apple gives the app an identifier that is unique to you and this app, and your name if you choose to share it. We don’t ask for your email address. Both are stored only on your device.
  • Your touch profile and keyboard settings are saved to your own iCloud (iCloud key-value storage), so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account can use them. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; we can’t see or access it.

Deleting your data

  • In the app, open Settings › Delete all data (or Delete account and data if you signed in). This removes your profile, settings, local activity history, medication schedule, saved phrases and recent spoken phrases, clears the synced profile and settings from iCloud, and signs you out.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.
  • To stop TypeParkinson using your Apple Account, open the Settings app › your name › Sign-In & Security › Sign in with Apple.

Children

TypeParkinson doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Health information

TypeParkinson describes how your touches behave. It isn’t a medical device, doesn’t assess or diagnose any condition, and isn’t a substitute for advice from a clinician. Your measurements are not sent to us. You may choose to export a report and share it with someone yourself.
