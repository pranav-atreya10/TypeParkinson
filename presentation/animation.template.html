TypeParkinson

A simple message, “See you soon,” slips onto neighboring keys. For people living with Parkinson's, tremor and stiffness can make each tap harder. An estimated 1.1 million people in the US live with Parkinson's, according to the Parkinson's Foundation.

A keyboard that learns your hands. Setup measures where your touches land. A personal touch model interprets the key you intended. On-device smart correction handles neighboring keys, repeated touches, missed letters and swaps. You can undo a correction with one tap.

Check in, practise, and find your voice. Daily checks, spiral tracing, rhythm tapping, typing, tremor measurement, voice practice, Speak and medication records live in one app. Motion sensors and Fourier analysis describe shake strength and frequency. Illustrations and simulator histories are sample data, not clinical evidence.

Patterns and PDF reports support conversations with your care team. Measurements do not diagnose a condition. No servers. The keyboard never saves what you type. Optional sync uses your own iCloud.

Built by Atharv Sharma and Pranav Atreya for the Congressional App Challenge.
